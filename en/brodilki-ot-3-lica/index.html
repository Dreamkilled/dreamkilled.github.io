Third person action games

• Tiny and Big in: Grandpa's leftovers

Cut ancient ruins with a laser. Return grandpa's underpants.

• Marble blast Ultra

Roll the marble ball.

• Kerbal Space Program

Build a rocket. Landed on the moon.

• Mount and Blade (1, Bannerlord)

Become the arena champion. Hire a squad. Conquer all cities.

• Kenshi

Become a hero of the wasteland. Build your own town and army.

• Overgrowth, Lugaru

You're a karate bunny.

• Clone Drone in the Danger Zone

For those who like to cut everyone with a laser sword.

• Toribash

Players control the characters' joints in turn-based brawls.

• Say No! More

By refusing, you become stronger.

• Ghost of a Tale

Mouse against rats.

• Grow home

Grow a giant stem and climb to the top. Game from Ubisoft.

• Alba: A Wildlife Adventure

Walk and take pictures of animals.

• Human Fall Flat

Clumsy dolls are trying to complete an obstacle course. Network co-op.

• Risk of Rain (1, 2)

A roguelike shooter with enhanced items. Co-op for four.

• Lil Gator Game

An adventure among cute animals.

• How Fish Is Made

The main thing is to decide up or down.

• Attack on Titan Tribute Game 2

Fan game based on the manga. There is multiplayer.

• V.A Proxy

Megastructures and battles with robots. You can also fend off a nuclear explosion.
