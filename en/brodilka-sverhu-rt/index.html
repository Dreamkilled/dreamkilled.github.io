There are no fights. Just a trip to the mountain and meeting people along the way.

The entire screen will drown in blood and debris. Lots of weapons and lots of enemies.

There are many enemies and they are zombies. Dark, bloody and not complicated.

The world restarts every sixty seconds.

Voxel enemies and environments.

• Hotline Miami (1, 2)

Music and fighting.

• Factorio

You will be surprised how complex factories you can build by solving one small problem after another.

• Stardew Valley

Adventures, mysticism and dates await you, and many, many garden beds.

• Monaco

Steal, run away. Gather a gang and commit the biggest theft in Monaco.

• CrossCode (CrossCode: A New Home)

A journey inside an MMO RPG.

• Yume Nikki

Walking through strange places in a dream.

• Space Station 13

Everyone works on the space station. Multiplayer drama generator.

Shootouts on spaceships. Complete the ship from the wreckage.

• Wizard of Legend

• Besiege

Eat others, grow big. Similar to the first stage in Spore.

• OneShot

One chance to re-ignite the sun.

You are an elemental mage. Elements interact! Raw characters are dried by fire, and frost turns them into ice. And don’t even try to cast lightning in a damp robe! There are even more unexpected interactions in co-op!

• Among Us

Multiplayer Mafia on a spaceship. Upbeat and dramatic. The game will teach you to lie shamelessly.

• Gang Beasts

A fight between four players. The players are clumsy bobbleheads.

• The Binding of Isaac

You shoot tears, you clear rooms. Bagel. Hundreds of strange artifacts.

• Cult of the Lamb

You manage a cult of cute animals and fight other cultists. There is a co-op.

• Mogeko Castle

• Yuppie Psycho

A creepy game about an office worker.

• Lamentum

Adventure in a Victorian mansion with monsters. Badihorror.

• Insatia

An ordinary snake, but against other snakes. You become not only longer, but also larger.
